In short

  • There is no account or sign-up.
  • Your journal, saved readings and settings stay on your phone.
  • When you create a Birth Chart & Patrika, the details you type (name, date, time and place of birth, language, and any questions) are sent securely to our server so the chart can be calculated and the written reading prepared.
  • When you compare with a friend, the two birth dates, times and places are sent to our server only to work out the chart positions for the match. They are not stored.
  • Share cards are made on your phone. Nothing is uploaded until you choose where to send the picture.
  • The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.
  • The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and sells optional purchases through Google Play.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your daily card, your chosen sun sign, your journal, and your saved Patrikas ("My Patrikas"), including the readings and answers. We cannot see these. You can remove them with Settings → Clear journal, by deleting a Patrika from My Patrikas, or by uninstalling the app.

What is sent to our server, and why

When you create a Patrika, the app sends your name, date, time and place of birth, reading language and (for the Complete Patrika) your questions. We use them only to calculate your chart and write your reading.

  • Not stored: we do not keep the details you typed after your reading is made. (A finished reading may mention your name; see "Temporary copy" below.)
  • Service provider: the text used to write your reading is processed by Anthropic (the Claude AI service) acting on our behalf. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this content to train its models, and it may keep it for a limited period under its own policies.
  • Temporary copy: for a Complete Patrika purchase, we keep the finished reading for up to 7 days so you can get it again if your connection drops. It is then deleted automatically.
  • Comparing with a friend: the birth details you enter for a friend are used only to calculate their chart positions for the match and are not stored. Please enter a friend's details only with their agreement.
  • Hosting: our server runs on Google Firebase (Google Cloud), in the Mumbai region (India).

Other information we keep

  • Install ID: a random identifier created by the app. It is used to limit daily use and to match watched videos and purchases to your phone. It is not your name, email or phone number. Counters are deleted after about 3 days, and video credits after about 30 days.
  • Purchases: purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive a purchase token to confirm the purchase and to make sure it is used once. We never see your card details. Purchase records hold only the token and its status (no name or birth details) and are deleted after about 13 months, so that each purchase can be used only once.

Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to show ads and to ask for consent where the law requires it. Google may collect and use, as described in Google's privacy policy:

  • your IP address (which can indicate approximate location)
  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID)
  • app interactions (such as ad views and taps)
  • app and SDK diagnostic and performance information

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where consent is required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and some other regions), you are asked for your choice and can change it in Settings → Ad privacy choices when that option appears. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Rewarded videos are confirmed by Google AdMob, which tells our server that a particular video was completed (using the random install ID and a one-time code, not any personal details).

Security

Data sent to our server travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

For data held by Google as an ad provider, use Google's own privacy controls.
