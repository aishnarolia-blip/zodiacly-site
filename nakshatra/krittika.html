Krittika Nakshatra: meaning, traits and details

Krittika cuts through confusion the way flame clears away what is not needed. It is linked with sharp judgment, ambition and a love of clarity. Natives are often honest to a fault and drawn to refining their craft.

Number3 of 27
SpansAries 26°40′ – Taurus 9°60′
Ruling planetSun
DeityAgni, the sacred fire
Symbola razor or flame
Gana (temperament)Rakshasa (fierce, strong-willed)
Yoni (animal)Sheep

Krittika sits in Aries, whose Vedic name is Mesha. See Mesha rashi to understand the sign it lives in.

Frequently asked questions

What is Krittika nakshatra?
Krittika is nakshatra number 3 of 27 in Vedic astrology. It spans Aries 26°40′ to Taurus 9°60′ of the sidereal zodiac, is ruled by Sun and is symbolised by a razor or flame.

Who is the deity of Krittika?
The presiding deity of Krittika is Agni, the sacred fire.

Which rashi does Krittika fall in?
Krittika falls in Aries (Mesha) and Taurus (Vrishabha).
