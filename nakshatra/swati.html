Swati Nakshatra: meaning, traits and details

Swati is independent and flexible, bending without breaking. It is associated with diplomacy, trade and a love of freedom. Natives tend to adapt quickly and prefer space to decide for themselves.

Number15 of 27
SpansLibra 6°40′ – Libra 19°60′
Ruling planetRahu
DeityVayu, the wind
Symbola young shoot swaying in the wind
Gana (temperament)Deva (gentle, light-hearted)
Yoni (animal)Buffalo

Swati and the Moon sign

Swati sits in Libra, whose Vedic name is Tula. See Tula rashi to understand the sign it lives in.

Frequently asked questions

What is Swati nakshatra?
Swati is nakshatra number 15 of 27 in Vedic astrology. It spans Libra 6°40′ to Libra 19°60′ of the sidereal zodiac, is ruled by Rahu and is symbolised by a young shoot swaying in the wind.

Who is the deity of Swati?
The presiding deity of Swati is Vayu, the wind.

Which rashi does Swati fall in?
Swati falls in Libra (Tula).
