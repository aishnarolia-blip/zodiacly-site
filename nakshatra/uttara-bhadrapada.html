Uttara Bhadrapada Nakshatra: meaning, traits and details

Uttara Bhadrapada is calm, deep and wise. It is linked with patience, compassion and a long view of life. Natives are often steadying presences who think before they act.

Number26 of 27
SpansPisces 3°20′ – Pisces 16°40′
Ruling planetSaturn
DeityAhir Budhnya, the serpent of the deep
Symbolthe back legs of a bed, or twins
Gana (temperament)Manushya (balanced, worldly)
Yoni (animal)Cow

Frequently asked questions

What is Uttara Bhadrapada nakshatra?
Uttara Bhadrapada is nakshatra number 26 of 27 in Vedic astrology. It spans Pisces 3°20′ to Pisces 16°40′ of the sidereal zodiac, is ruled by Saturn and is symbolised by the back legs of a bed, or twins.

Who is the deity of Uttara Bhadrapada?
The presiding deity of Uttara Bhadrapada is Ahir Budhnya, the serpent of the deep.

Which rashi does Uttara Bhadrapada fall in?
Uttara Bhadrapada falls in Pisces (Meena).
