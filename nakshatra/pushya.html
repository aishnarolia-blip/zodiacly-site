Pushya Nakshatra: meaning, traits and details

Often called the most nourishing nakshatra, Pushya is about care, guidance and steady support. It is linked with duty and generosity. Teachers, carers and quiet pillars of a family often carry this star.

Number8 of 27
SpansCancer 3°20′ – Cancer 16°40′
Ruling planetSaturn
DeityBrihaspati, teacher of the gods
Symbola cow’s udder or lotus
Gana (temperament)Deva (gentle, light-hearted)
Yoni (animal)Sheep

How to find your nakshatra

Your birth nakshatra is the one the Moon was in when you were born. It depends on your exact birth date, time and place, so a free Vedic birth chart calculator is the quickest way to find it.

Pushya and the Moon sign

Pushya sits in Cancer, whose Vedic name is Karka. See Karka rashi to understand the sign it lives in.

Frequently asked questions

What is Pushya nakshatra?
Pushya is nakshatra number 8 of 27 in Vedic astrology. It spans Cancer 3°20′ to Cancer 16°40′ of the sidereal zodiac, is ruled by Saturn and is symbolised by a cow’s udder or lotus.

Who is the deity of Pushya?
The presiding deity of Pushya is Brihaspati, teacher of the gods.

Which rashi does Pushya fall in?
Pushya falls in Cancer (Karka).
