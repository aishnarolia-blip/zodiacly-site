Purva Bhadrapada Nakshatra: meaning, traits and details

Purva Bhadrapada is idealistic and intense, driven to transform things. It is associated with conviction, imagination and unconventional paths. They tend to follow a vision, often one that is bigger than themselves.

Number25 of 27
SpansAquarius 20°00′ – Pisces 3°20′
Ruling planetJupiter
DeityAja Ekapada, a fiery form of the sun
Symbola sword, or a two-faced figure
Gana (temperament)Manushya (balanced, worldly)
Yoni (animal)Lion

Frequently asked questions

What is Purva Bhadrapada nakshatra?
Purva Bhadrapada is nakshatra number 25 of 27 in Vedic astrology. It spans Aquarius 20°00′ to Pisces 3°20′ of the sidereal zodiac, is ruled by Jupiter and is symbolised by a sword, or a two-faced figure.

Who is the deity of Purva Bhadrapada?
The presiding deity of Purva Bhadrapada is Aja Ekapada, a fiery form of the sun.

Which rashi does Purva Bhadrapada fall in?
Purva Bhadrapada falls in Aquarius (Kumbha) and Pisces (Meena).
