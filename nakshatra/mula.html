Mula Nakshatra: meaning, traits and details

Mula goes to the root of things. It is associated with research, honesty and a willingness to question what is taken for granted. They tend to seek the truth, even when it means starting over.

Number19 of 27
SpansSagittarius 0°00′ – Sagittarius 13°20′
Ruling planetKetu
DeityNirriti, goddess of dissolution
Symbola bunch of roots
Gana (temperament)Rakshasa (fierce, strong-willed)
Yoni (animal)Dog

How to find your nakshatra

Mula and the Moon sign

Mula sits in Sagittarius, whose Vedic name is Dhanu. See Dhanu rashi to understand the sign it lives in.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mula nakshatra?
Mula is nakshatra number 19 of 27 in Vedic astrology. It spans Sagittarius 0°00′ to Sagittarius 13°20′ of the sidereal zodiac, is ruled by Ketu and is symbolised by a bunch of roots.

Who is the deity of Mula?
The presiding deity of Mula is Nirriti, goddess of dissolution.

Which rashi does Mula fall in?
Mula falls in Sagittarius (Dhanu).
