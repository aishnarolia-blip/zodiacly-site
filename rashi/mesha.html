Vedic sign vs Western sign

Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac, aligned to the fixed stars. Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac, aligned to the seasons. The two have drifted about 24° apart, so for most people the Vedic Sun sign is the one before the Western sign. Your Rashi in Vedic astrology is your Moon sign, not your Sun sign.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mesha the same as Aries?
Mesha is the Vedic (sidereal) name for the sign Western astrology calls Aries. Because the Vedic zodiac is about 24° behind the Western one, your Vedic sign is often the sign before your Western one.

What is the ruling planet of Mesha?
Mesha is ruled by Mars. Its element is Fire and its quality is movable.
